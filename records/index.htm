is Kait's record label, originally meant for "serious" records as opposed to the "unserious" SBR. Also releasing records by my pals. More song-based work goes here, though it doesn't really matter, does it?

 

SBR

was Kait's first record label. A mismanaged mess that released all sorts of things by all sorts of people.

Existed from 2014-2022.

 

NO THUMB
